Policy status

Policies are not published yet.

This page is a status notice, not a Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, refund policy, or legal agreement.

ZervxHub is not open as a production or paid service.

Do not send payment or submit sensitive personal information. No official terms, privacy notice, cancellation/refund policy, or formal support commitment has been published.

Data handled by a configured deployment

The repository code processes Discord user and guild IDs, Roblox user IDs and Universe IDs, developer/game records, hashed license and API credentials, and key lifecycle timestamps. A deployment stores operational records in its configured D1 database. The website itself has no account form or analytics code, but a hosting provider may independently process access logs under its own policies.

Data retention periods, data-subject request handling, deletion workflows, and a privacy contact have not been established here. Operators must document and review these before production use.

Before a commercial launch

  • Publish accurate terms, privacy, refund, cancellation, and support policies reviewed for the seller's operating jurisdictions.
  • Document what data is collected, why it is used, how long it is retained, and how users can request access or deletion where required.
  • Define a monitored support channel and incident-response process.
  • Do not accept payments until billing, customer entitlements, payment-provider security, and policy workflows are implemented and tested.

For current product scope, see pricing and access status and the repository's commercial-readiness plan.
